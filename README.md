# Aaimee Gogoi — Totoro-inspired Data Science & AI Portfolio

## Open the website
1. Keep the `assets` folder beside `index.html`.
2. Double-click `index.html`.
3. It will open in Chrome and work locally.

## Your two artworks
- `assets/totoro-forest.webp` — green forest hero
- `assets/totoro-night.jpeg` — night transition section

## Add your information later

### GitHub
Already connected to:
`https://github.com/AAIMEE-GOGOI`

### LinkedIn
Open `index.html`, find the LinkedIn contact link, and replace `href="#"` with your LinkedIn URL.

### Email
Find `your-email@example.com` in `index.html` and replace it with your real email.

### Resume
When you create your resume:
1. Put the PDF in `assets/`
2. Name it `resume.pdf`
3. Change the Resume link in `index.html` to `assets/resume.pdf`

### Certificates
In the Certificates section, replace the three placeholder cards with your certificate name, issuer, date and link.

### Projects
In the Projects section, replace "Coming Soon" cards with your real projects. You can add GitHub/demo links to each card.

## Main files
- `index.html` — content
- `style.css` — design and responsive layout
- `script.js` — navigation, leaves and scroll animations
- `assets/` — artwork and future resume/certificate files

No framework or installation is required.
